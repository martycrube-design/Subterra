Subterra for macOS
==================

Easiest: double-click Subterra-macOS-Installer.pkg and follow the steps.
It installs:
  Subterra.component  ->  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (Audio Unit: Logic, GarageBand, Ableton Live)
  Subterra.vst3       ->  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3          (VST3: Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig)
  Subterra.app        ->  /Applications                         (standalone app)

If macOS says it can't verify the developer, right-click the .pkg, choose Open, then Open again.
(Or open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".)

Then restart your DAW and rescan plugins. Subterra appears as an audio effect from "Subterra Audio".
Put it on the track your bass is recorded or monitored on.

Manual install (zip): copy the files to the folders above, then run this once in Terminal,
because files copied by hand keep the download quarantine:

  sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Subterra.component /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Subterra.vst3 /Applications/Subterra.app
