Subterra for Windows
====================

1. Copy Subterra.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. Subterra.exe is the standalone app (optional): pick your audio interface under Options.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. Subterra appears as an audio effect from "Subterra Audio".
   Put it on the track your bass is recorded or monitored on.
